      FAQ

      Fair questions.

      Will this actually sound like my business?

      Yes. Every assistant is trained on your services, prices, hours, and tone of voice. Customers won't know it's AI unless you tell them.

      What if it gives a wrong answer?

      It only answers from information you approve. Anything it's unsure about gets escalated to you or your team instantly — never guessed.

      Do I need new software or hardware?

      No. It plugs into the website, phone number, and calendar you already have. Nothing to install, nothing to learn.

      What happens after the free pilot?

      You get a report: calls answered, bookings made, reviews collected. If the numbers don't impress you, we remove everything. No charge, no hard feelings.

      Why local businesses?

      Because that's where the pain is worst — missed calls, no-shows, dead lead lists — and where automation pays for itself fastest.

      How long does setup take?

      We build and launch your pilot within 7 days — you approve everything before it goes live.

      Am I locked into a contract?

      No. The pilot is free, Launch is one-time, and Growth is month-to-month. Cancel anytime.

      Which industries does this work for?

      Any business that takes calls, books appointments, or chases leads — restaurants, salons, plumbers, dentists, auto shops, contractors, clinics, and more. Try the demo above in your industry.

      I already have front-desk staff. Why do I need this?

      Flo doesn't replace your team — it covers what they can't: nights, weekends, lunch rushes, and the 3rd call ringing while they're with a customer. Think of it as the employee who never sleeps.
